Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how BlockSpin Values (blockspinvalues.com) uses cookies and similar technologies. It should be read with our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Similar technologies include local storage and pixels used by analytics or advertising partners.

2. How We Use Them

  • Essential: Needed for basic site features (for example remembering cookie consent, login session support, and preferences such as theme or language).
  • Analytics: Help us understand site traffic and usage (for example Google Analytics), only if you Accept.
  • Advertising: Used by ad partners to show and measure ads, only if you Accept.

3. Partners That May Set Cookies

  • Cloudflare: CDN and security for the website. May set cookies needed for performance, security, or bot protection.
  • Google Analytics: Site analytics, if you Accept cookies.
  • Monetag: Advertising (for example vignette and in-page push), if you Accept cookies.
  • Adsterra: Advertising (display and native banners), if you Accept cookies.

Those partners process data under their own policies.

4. Your Choices

When you first visit, you can Accept or Reject analytics and advertising cookies. Essential cookies may still be used so the site works.
